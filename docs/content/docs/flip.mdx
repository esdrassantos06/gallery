---
title: Flip
description: Mirror images horizontally or vertically, composing with rotation and zoom.
---

```tsx
<Lightbox flip rotate {...props} />
```

`flip` adds flip horizontally and vertically buttons for images. It is off by default.

<Demo count={4} flip rotate />

- A flip always mirrors across the screen axis named on the button. On a rotated image, where the image's own axes are swapped, it still reads the way the button says.
- Flips compose with rotation and zoom, and animate on `transform`.
- They reset on slide change, when the viewer closes and with the `0` key, which also resets zoom and rotation.
- The buttons are named by `labels.flipHorizontal` and `labels.flipVertical`, and use `icons.flipHorizontal` and `icons.flipVertical`.
- On a narrow screen they can move into the [overflow menu](/docs/toolbar).
