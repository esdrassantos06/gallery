---
title: Right-to-left
description: Mirror arrows, swipes, keys, thumbnails, dots and toolbar for right-to-left languages.
---

The lightbox follows the direction of the element it is portalled into, so `<html dir="rtl">` is enough. `dir="rtl"` or `dir="ltr"` forces one.

```tsx
<Lightbox dir="rtl" labels={arabicLabels} {...props} />
```

<Demo count={5} dir="rtl" pager />

In right-to-left layouts, the next slide comes from the left:

- The arrows swap sides and their icons mirror.
- A drag to the right and `ArrowLeft` go forward.
- The thumbnails, dots and toolbar run right to left.
- The [overflow menu](/docs/toolbar) opens from the left edge.
- The slideshow progress grows from the right.

The root carries `dir="ltr"` or `dir="rtl"`, so your CSS can target it. Translate the strings with [`labels`](/docs/labels).
