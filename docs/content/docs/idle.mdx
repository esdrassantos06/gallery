---
title: Idle controls
description: Fade the controls out after a pause and bring them back on any input.
---

```tsx
<Lightbox idle {...props} />
<Lightbox idle={5000} {...props} />
```

With `idle`, the top bar, arrows, caption, thumbnails and pager fade out after 3 seconds, or the number of ms you pass, without pointer movement, touch, wheel or key presses. The pointer is hidden over the image too. It is off by default.

Open the demo and keep still for two seconds.

<Demo count={4} idle={2000} />

- Any of those inputs brings the controls back, and the tap that wakes them never closes the viewer.
- They stay while a control has keyboard focus, while the pointer rests on one and while the [overflow menu](/docs/toolbar) is open.
- Hidden controls are still reachable with Tab, which shows them again.
- The fade uses `opacity` and `--eg-duration`, so it is instant under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- The root gets `data-idle` while the controls are hidden.
